About

Simone Falcomer is a photographer and art director based in Barcelona.

Born in Italy, he studied photojournalism at Grisart Academy in Barcelona, where he developed a solid and personal approach to the image. He is currently studying Fine Arts in Barcelona while working as a freelance photographer across fashion, documentary and artistic practice.

His work is in constant evolution — each project brings him into unknown territory, always outside his comfort zone. He shoots on analogue film, working in 35mm and medium format, and uses photography as a tool, believing his fullest expression is still ahead of him.

Through the lens, Simone explores the passage of time, reflecting on its relativity and on the inner and outer transformations of himself, of others, and of the world. Drawing inspiration from mid-20th-century photography and Baroque art, his visual language seeks to expose the pathos of contemporary existence: the unease, fragility and contradictions of our time, beneath an era increasingly shaped by artificial values and a fabricated sense of ethos. At its core is a search for human error.
